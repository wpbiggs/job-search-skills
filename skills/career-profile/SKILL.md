# Career profile

## Inputs
An approved private workspace path, the candidate's own resume or experience notes, and their permission to read them. No browser is needed.

## Workflow
1. Read existing `profile.md` and `resume.md`. If missing, ask the candidate to initialize a workspace or create private drafts with permission.
2. Identify missing hard constraints: location, work arrangement, compensation, employment type, travel, schedule, and authorization needs. Ask only a few high-impact questions at a time; sensitive disclosures are optional.
3. Elicit work the person enjoys, wants to avoid, and wants to learn. Do not reduce suitability to the most recent job title.
4. Build an evidence inventory of actual accomplishments, transferable skills, project contributions, and credentials. Mark unverified metrics rather than filling them in.
5. Propose a small set of target role families with evidence, likely gaps, and trade-offs. Ask the candidate to confirm priorities.
6. With permission, update `profile.md`, preserving their wording and separating confirmed facts from open questions. Do not alter the factual base resume without approval.

## Output
A confirmed search brief: role families, must-haves, preferences, exclusions, evidence inventory, unknowns, and agreed search scope. Hand off to job-discovery.

## Safety
Keep data private and outside the public repository. Never invent qualifications or infer sensitive attributes. Ask before disclosing private content to an external model or site. Read the repository's AGENTS.md when available; these rules still apply when the skill is installed alone.
